Paper Empires Publisher is an internal, non-commercial tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Paper Empires (@thepaperempires_co) to publish and schedule the channel's own videos on its own accounts and to read their statistics. It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else.

Use of the tool is subject to the terms of each platform it connects to: the YouTube Terms of Service, the TikTok Terms of Service and the Meta Terms of Service. Only content owned by the channel, or licensed for its use, is published.

Every publication is reviewed by the owner, who chooses its visibility and settings and confirms it before it is sent to the platform.
